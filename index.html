Monster Mixer

Build silly monsters and count their legs. For ages 4 to 7, on iPhone and iPad

Your child picks a body, a head, legs, wings, a tail, a pattern and a color. The monster comes alive right away: new legs take a few steps, new wings flap, a new head giggles. Tap it and it hops, sneezes or flies.

You pay once. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. The app works offline and collects no data.

What it does

Make a monster.
Tap a picture on the side to try the next head, legs or wings, or swipe across that part of the monster. The dice button makes a surprise monster. The heart sends it to the zoo.
Orders: count what the voice asks for.
A voice asks for a monster: "It needs 6 legs and 2 wings. Make it blue!" The order is also drawn, as a number and that many little legs or eyes. When your child taps the check mark, the app looks at the monster. If something is missing, the voice says what to count again and the right picture glows. There are no red marks, no buzzers and no timer.
The zoo.
Every saved monster gets a silly name like Wobble-Zip and strolls around the zoo with the others. Tap one to hear its name.
49 parts and 8 colors.
8 bodies, 10 heads with one, two or three eyes, 8 sets of legs (2, 4, 6 or 8), 7 kinds of wings, 8 tails and 8 patterns. That makes millions of different monsters, all friendly.
Grows with your child.
In the grown-ups section pick age 4, 5 or 6–7. Orders then ask for one, two or three things and count up to 4, 6 or 8.
Speaks your language.
The voice uses the language of your device: English, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Polish, Turkish and Dutch.

Questions

How do I get to the grown-ups section?
Tap "Grown-ups" on the home screen and type the number shown in words, for example "forty-seven", using digits. A new number appears after a wrong answer.
There is no voice.
Check that Voice is on in the grown-ups section and that the volume is up. The voice plays even with the silent switch on. If your device has no voice for its language, the app speaks English.
The voice sounds robotic.
The app uses the voices built into iOS. You can download nicer ones: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
The orders are too easy or too hard.
Change the age in the grown-ups section. The next order uses the new setting.
How do I remove monsters from the zoo?
In the grown-ups section, swipe a monster to the left to delete it, or use "Delete all monsters". The zoo shows the 30 newest monsters.
Can the whole family use it?
Yes. Family Sharing is on, so everyone in your family group can download it at no extra cost.
