Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Monster Mixer does not collect personal information from anyone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no network requests made by the app.

What stays on your device

The monsters in the zoo, the number of finished orders and the settings (age, voice, sounds, vibration) are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

The voice

The spoken words come from the speech voices built into iOS and are produced on the device. Nothing is recorded, and the app never uses the microphone or the camera.

Children's privacy

Monster Mixer is made for children aged 4 to 7 and is offered in the Kids Category of the App Store. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or use for advertising.

  • The app never asks for a name, photo, voice recording or location. Monster names are made up by the app.
  • No third party receives any information from the app. It contains no ads, analytics or other third-party code.
  • Settings and all links to outside websites are in the grown-ups section behind a parental gate, so a child cannot reach them by accident.
  • To delete your child's monsters, use "Delete all monsters" in the grown-ups section, or delete the app.

Buying the app

Monster Mixer is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details or Apple Account. There are no purchases inside the app.

If you write to us

If you email us, we see your email address and message and use them only to answer you.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date. Questions: shchukavlad4@gmail.com.
